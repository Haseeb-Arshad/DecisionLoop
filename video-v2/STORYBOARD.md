# Storyboard: "The Line on the Wall"

**Idea:** every decision rests on something believed to be true. When that stops being true, the decisions
keep running and nobody connects the two. DecisionLoop does.

**Motif:** one dashed line, the assumption. A flood mark on a river wall, then the threshold in the engine,
then the line under every kind of decision. When reality rises above it, a person is needed.

Approved 2026-10-01. 30 fps, 1920 × 1080. Neighbouring scenes cross-fade over 15 frames.
